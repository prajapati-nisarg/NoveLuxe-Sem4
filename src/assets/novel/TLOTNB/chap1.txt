Chapter 1: Standing Alone Under a Fallen Sky

Jin Muwon opened his eyes. The world seemed strangely blurry, probably because of the tears welling up. Despite trying to hold them back, tears kept flowing uncontrollably. Jin Muwon wiped his eyes with his sleeve.

"I'll cry only until today. From now on, I won't shed any more tears."

At thirteen years old, this was the vow of a still young boy.

Just then, someone reached out and gently patted Jin Muwon's head. Jin Muwon looked up at him. The man's face, usually wearing a relaxed smile, now carried a solemn atmosphere.

He knelt down to meet Jin Muwon's gaze.

"Son, from now on, you're on your own."

"Father."

"I'm sorry."

"You know you're sorry?"

The man nodded in response to Jin Muwon's words. He placed his large hand on Jin Muwon's shoulders.

"You know, right? From now on, you are the leader of the Northern Heavenly Gate."

Jin Muwon nodded silently. The man patted Jin Muwon's trembling shoulders. Despite his maturity, Jin Muwon was only thirteen, still an age needing parental protection.

Jin Muwon forced himself to look away, staring into the void to avoid his father's eyes.

"Tch! Being the leader of a crumbling sect, what kind of inheritance is this?"

"I'm sorry."

"Don't say sorry. Apologies don't suit you, Father."

"Right, I forgot who I was for a moment."

The man stood up straight. His name was Jin Gwanho, known as the Northern Wall. To some, he was a wall of despair, to others, a strong shield. Apologies didn't suit him.

"Blame me for this. I'm the one who let things get this bad."

"It's fine. It's actually good. No need to be tied down by grand causes anymore. I'm free."

"Thank you for thinking that way."

Jin Gwanho nodded and looked outside. The wide training ground was filled with people, all waiting for Jin Gwanho to come out.

"Is it time to meet expectations? Might as well make it grand, right?"

Jin Muwon's eyes wavered at Jin Gwanho's calm words. He bit his lip hard. Even as blood trickled down, Jin Muwon seemed not to feel the pain, only watching Jin Gwanho.

"Father."

Jin Muwon watched as Jin Gwanho walked out ahead of him. His father's broad and strong back was imprinted in Jin Muwon's eyes.

Following Jin Gwanho, Jin Muwon saw the people gathered in the training ground. People of all ages, wearing various outfits, but all with intense eyes, showing they were martial artists.

The few remaining warriors of the Northern Heavenly Gate stood in front of them, looking as fragile as candles in a storm.

Their sharp gazes were fixed on Jin Gwanho. Jin Gwanho looked at each of them. Those who met his eyes turned away as if guilty. Some, however, glared at him with open hostility. Among them were people Jin Gwanho and Jin Muwon had known for a long time.

Jin Gwanho murmured as he looked at them.

"It seems all the martial artists of the world have gathered. Should I be honored?"

It seemed the top martial artists of the current world had gathered. Among them, nine stood out, ranging from a martial artist in their mid-thirties to an elderly monk in their late seventies. Their presence naturally overwhelmed the crowd.

It was an aura only those who stood above all others could possess. Jin Gwanho's expression turned cold as he looked at them.

"So, this was all Yun Jungcheon’s doing. I can't help but be impressed."

"Master Jin."

An elderly man, appearing in his early sixties, stepped forward. His appearance was ordinary, but his eyes shone with a penetrating light, making most people uncomfortable to stand before him.

"The famous Ghost Zhuge, Elder of the Seomun family."

Jin Gwanho immediately recognized the old man's identity. His name was Seomunhwa, a genius who revived the fallen Seomun family. His knowledge and insight were so great that it was said all the truths of the world were contained in his small head. After passing the family head position to his son, he became an elder and one of the Nine Heavens of Yun Jungcheon. Today, Seomunhwa appeared at the Northern Heavenly Gate with the Nine Heavens.

"Why did you do it, Master Jin?"

"I'm too foolish to understand what Elder Seomun is saying."

"Are you really denying what you did? Instead of stopping the Night of Secrets, you colluded with them."

"Haha! I’ve lived my life without shame. To say I colluded with the Night of Secrets is nonsense."

"The Northern Heavenly Gate's four pillars testified against you. Are you still going to deny it?"

Jin Gwanho's gaze turned to four men standing behind the crowd. Though obscured, Jin Gwanho could clearly feel their presence.

"Are you too ashamed to show your face?"

The Northern Heavenly Gate's four pillars. Once Jin Gwanho's friends, they were the shield and spear of the Northern Heavenly Gate, trusted and relied upon. Even they turned their backs on Jin Gwanho and the Northern Heavenly Gate. Many others followed their betrayal.

"Completely alone now."

Suddenly, his gaze turned to Jin Muwon, standing quietly beside him. His son was calm and composed, but Jin Gwanho knew it was just a facade. Jin Muwon's trembling shoulders proved it. At thirteen, enduring and accepting such sudden misfortune was too much.

Jin Gwanho placed a hand on Jin Muwon's shoulder. Feeling his father's warmth, Jin Muwon looked up.

His son's dark eyes said so much.

"I'm sorry, son."

Leaving his son behind, Jin Gwanho walked toward Seomunhwa. The Nine Heavens, including Seomunhwa, flinched.

"No matter how much I struggle, there’s no escape from the trap you've set. But this is the Northern Heavenly Gate. If I wanted, a third of the martial artists here wouldn’t survive."

"So you’re going to resist, Master Jin?"

For the first time, tension appeared on Seomunhwa's face, and others felt it too. Even facing downfall, this was the Northern Heavenly Gate's stronghold. Considering they had held off the Night of Secrets for a hundred years, it wouldn’t be surprising if there were deadly traps and mechanisms here. A third of the gathered martial artists might indeed lose their lives.

Especially Jin Gwanho, the man known as the Northern Wall. If he charged with the intent to die, the damage would be unimaginable.

Though the four pillars turned their backs on him, many elite warriors of the Northern Heavenly Gate still followed Jin Gwanho. If he commanded, they would charge without fear of death.

This heightened the tension among Seomunhwa and the Nine Heavens. But Jin Gwanho's next words were unexpected.

"I will disband the Northern Heavenly Gate."

"Really?"

"I have no reason to lie now."

Jin Gwanho smiled at the suspicious Seomunhwa. His knowing look made the Nine Heavens’ faces flush with shame. Jin Gwanho was not an easy person to deal with. Even for a moment, the Nine Heavens felt intimidated by him.

"The Northern Wall, a man who must be eliminated."

"As long as he exists, we cannot control the Central Plains as we wish. He must be removed."

The Nine Heavens exchanged secret glances. Whether Jin Gwanho knew their intentions or not, he turned and shouted.

"From today, the Northern Heavenly Gate is disbanded! All disciples are free to leave and live as they wish! This is my final order as the gate master!"

"Master!"

"Ugh!"

Jin Gwanho's thunderous declaration caused the warriors of the Northern Heavenly Gate, who were prepared to die, to collapse. Tears flowed freely from their faces.

Jin Gwanho looked back at Seomunhwa and the others.

"Is it enough now?"

"……."

"It seems not."

Jin Gwanho's smile deepened. The answer they wanted was predetermined. Nothing else would satisfy their desires.

Jin Gwanho gathered his inner strength. The air around him trembled, and sharp winds spread in all directions. Seeing this, Seomunhwa and the Nine Heavens also gathered their inner strength, ready to attack if necessary.

At the peak of his power, Jin Gwanho suddenly reversed his energy flow. With a loud sound, his body shook violently.

"Father!"

Jin Muwon ran to the collapsing Jin Gwanho. In Jin Muwon's arms, Jin Gwanho's mouth and nose bled dark red.

"Ugh!"

Everyone knew what had happened. Jin Gwanho's internal channels had burst from the forceful reversal of his energy. With all his channels destroyed, even a great immortal could not save him.

Jin Gwanho looked at Seomunhwa and the others with bloodshot eyes.

"Is it enough now?"

The martial artists, including Seomunhwa, were speechless. A man who could have resisted chose to end his own life. His extreme choice left everyone silent, and the sharp aura of the martial artists diminished. Seomunhwa bit his lip.

His gaze turned to Jin Muwon, holding Jin Gwanho.

"I should have taken the opportunity to eradicate the roots."

But the atmosphere made that impossible. Pushing further would only breed resentment among the martial artists.

"Everyone, withdraw!"

At Seomunhwa's command, Jin Gwanho, in Jin Muwon's arms, smiled weakly. His face, already devoid of life, was kept alive only by his strong inner strength. But even that had reached its limit.

"I'm sorry, son."

"Father."

"Live freely."

Jin Gwanho passed away with a smile on his face. Jin Muwon looked at his father's face for a long time. Despite ending his own life, Jin Gwanho's face looked peaceful. Jin Muwon gently closed his father's eyes with trembling hands.

Holding his father's cold body, Jin Muwon turned around. From now on, he was alone. He had to stand alone under a fallen sky.

The martial artists silently watched Jin Muwon's long, dark shadow. Even in the face of his father's death, Jin Muwon's tearless demeanor sent a strange shiver through them.
